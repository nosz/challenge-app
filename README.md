## 🇬🇧 English

Welcome to the Challenge App 💪 your daily companion for new challenges!

A motivating app that helps you set, track, and achieve your daily goals and personal challenges, one step at a time. Master your goals step by step and grow beyond yourself — with success messages that actually feel good.

### ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Daily challenges** — define a personal goal and track your progress day by day
- 📈 **Progress overview** — see your streak and history at a glance
- 💬 **Motivating messages** — encouraging quotes and completion messages, fully customizable
- 🎨 **Color themes** — several light and dark themes to choose from
- 🌍 **Multi-language** — available in German, English and Polish
- 📱 **Installable PWA** — add it to your home screen and use it like a native app
- 🔌 **Works offline** — no internet connection required once installed
- 🔒 **No login, no tracking** — your data stays completely private, stored locally on your phone or PC only

### 🚀 Getting started

Just open the app in your browser — no installation, no account required:

**👉 [nosz.github.io/challenge-app](https://nosz.github.io/challenge-app)**

To install it as an app, open the link on your phone or desktop and choose **"Add to Home Screen"** (mobile) or **"Install app"** (desktop browser).

### 🔐 Privacy

Your data stays completely private — everything is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is ever sent to a server.
